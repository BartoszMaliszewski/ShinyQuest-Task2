The Shiny Charm adds three extra shiny rolls for eligible wild spawns. Each new Pokemon spawn is a separate check. Enter the number of eligible spawns you've checked, not just the number of resets.

A 50% cumulative chance does not guarantee a shiny. Odds do not improve just because you've been unlucky.

The Pokédex covers base-game Lumiose species. Mega Dimension DLC exclusives and Sparkling Power methods are not included yet. Fossil restoration uses base odds even with the Shiny Charm.

Rules reference: Serebii Z-A shiny guide.
